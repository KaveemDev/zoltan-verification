This Travel Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Zoltan Travel-Visas (hereinafter referred to as the "Company") and the undersigned individual (hereinafter referred to as the "Client"), collectively referred to as the "Parties."

1. Processing Timeline

1.1 The standard processing time for travel-related services shall be 35 days, with an additional grace period of 10 days.

1.2 Should the process exceed the stipulated timeline, a penalty fee of INR 5000 per exceeded month will be applicable.

2. Document Submission & Compliance

2.1 The Client shall submit all required documents, including but not limited to passports, identification documents, and visa-related paperwork, immediately upon request by the Company.

2.2 The Client must submit a bank statement with a sufficient balance within three (3) months from the initiation of the process. Failure to do so will result in the automatic dissolution of the case, without any entitlement to a refund or further assistance.

2.3 The Client bears full responsibility for ensuring the accuracy, completeness, and authenticity of all submitted documents.

3. Bank Statement Requirement

3.1 Embassies typically require 3 to 6 months of bank statements reflecting a well-maintained closing balance.

3.2 The Company will upload documents as received but will not be responsible for arranging, modifying, or assisting in furnishing bank statements under any circumstances.

4. Refund & Termination Policy

4.1 Once the travel process is initiated, no refunds shall be granted under any circumstances.

4.2 The Client may choose to terminate the process at their discretion; however, no refunds, reimbursements, or compensations shall be issued for any reason.

5. Hotel & Flight Bookings

5.1 Hotel and flight bookings shall be provisional and subject to availability.

5.2 The Client must provide a credit or debit card with international transaction capabilities for bookings.

5.3 Confirmed bookings are subject to additional charges, which will be borne by the Client.

6. Document Standards, Submission & Collection

6.1 The Client must provide scanned copies of all required documents, as embassies strictly accept only digital submissions.

6.2 The Company shall not be held responsible for visa refusals due to poor-quality scans or non-compliance with document submission standards.

6.3 Photographs must adhere to the official visa specifications; refusals due to improper or unclear photographs will not be the responsibility of the Company.

6.4 Embassies reserve the right to request additional documentation before or after submission, and the Client is obligated to provide such documents promptly.

6.5 The Client must collect all submitted documents at the time of submission or within one (1) month of submission. The Company shall not be responsible for any documents beyond this period.

7. Submission, Processing & Decision Authority

7.1 Prior to submission, the Company will conduct a final cross-verification of the Client’s application.

7.2 Once an application has been submitted, no modifications or additions can be made.

7.3 The embassy retains sole discretion in the approval or rejection of visa applications. The Company has no control over the outcome and shall not be held liable for any refusals, whether justified or unjustified.
